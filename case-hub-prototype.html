Expert UX Review: Kya Ye Sahi Hai?

  • Haan, ye 100% sahi aur modern approach hai: Sidebar me 3 alag-alag tabs rakhne se clutter hota hai. 1 unified "Case Operations" ya "Case Registry" tab hona bahut clean aur fast hota hai.
  • Search Box Common ho jayega: Abhi aapko Update me alag search krna padta hai aur Delete me alag. Ek hi jagah hone se user ek hi case ko load karke edit ya delete dono kar sakta hai.
  • Delete ko Danger Zone / Confirmation me hi rakhein: Delete option ko humesha alag tab me ya conditional red card me rakhna chahiye taaki galti se koi case delete na ho jaye (jaisa is prototype me dikhaya hai).
